## What Is This System?

The AI Dashboard is a **personal AI assistant** that lives on your organization's computer. It can:

| Capability | What It Means For You |
|-------------|----------------------|
| **Chat** | Ask questions and get instant answers |
| **Reports** | Receive daily briefings on important topics |
| **Tasks** | Have the AI monitor and track things for you |
| **Documents** | Upload files and ask questions about them |
| **Brands** | Get responses in your organization's voice |
| **Memory** | Remembers important context across conversations |
| **Security** | Automatically checks for vulnerabilities |

**Your data stays private** - Unlike cloud AI services, everything runs on your own hardware.

## Quick Start for Busy Operators

### To Get Your Daily Briefing

1. Open the AI Dashboard in your browser
2. Look for the **"Briefing"** button in the header (indigo colored)
3. Click it to see your daily summary

### To Ask a Question

1. Type your question in the chat box at the bottom
2. Press Enter or click Send
3. Wait for the response

### To Upload a Document

1. Click the **paperclip icon** in the chat area
2. Select your file (PDF, Word, or text files)
3. Ask questions about the document

## Key Concepts

### What is a "Briefing"?

A briefing is your daily summary from the AI system. It includes:

• **Intelligence** - News and updates relevant to your interests  
• **Tasks** - What's pending and what's been completed  
• **Events** - Upcoming calendar items  
• **Security** - Any warnings or recommended actions  
• **Learnings** - New information the AI has remembered  

Think of it as your morning newspaper, customized by and for you.

### What is "Context"?

When you upload documents or select a brand, you're giving the AI **context**. This helps it give better answers that match your specific situation.

Example: Without context, asking "What are our Q3 results?" might give generic advice. With your documents uploaded, it can analyze your actual Q3 results.

### What is "Memory"?

The system has a persistent memory. It remembers:

• Your name and preferences  
• Your organization's guidelines  
• Previous conversations and decisions  
• Important facts you've shared  

This means you don't have to repeat yourself every time you chat.

## Safety and Security

**Important things to know:**

1. **Your data is local** - It never leaves your organization's computer unless you explicitly ask it to
2. **Security scans run automatically** - The system checks itself for vulnerabilities
3. **You control access** - Only people with access to the computer can use the system
4. **Audit trail** - All activities are logged
